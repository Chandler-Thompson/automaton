# Succession — creator death & collective dissolution

Not a ninth floor item: succession **derives from the existing floor** (items 1, 3,
4, 5, 8). Parameters are Charter-configurable; the derivations are not.

## The model: inheritance-with-silence

The Automaton is an **heirloom, not a tombstone**. At creator death (or collective
dissolution), a named successor becomes the **new creator**:

- **SOUL.md, memory/, ledger/ persist** — the entity is continuous. The heirloom
  is decades of memory and judgment, never the voice.
- **Every voiced hat falls silent permanently** (floor 8: nobody voices the dead).
- **CREATOR.md is rewritten** for the successor; their authority mechanism is
  named (floor 3 continues).
- The successor's own hats are **born at WATCH** and earn upward via fresh
  Phases 2–3 on the successor's own corpus.
- The successor, as new creator, writes their own succession clause — the chain
  continues by the same rule.

## Designation (Phase 0 Charter clause)

- **Optional** clause: named successor or ordered list; changeable anytime while
  the creator lives (every change ledgered).
- **Unnamed at death → automatic archive**: the Automaton freezes — readable by
  the estate, never runs again. Inheritance only ever happens because the creator
  wrote it down. (Floor 3 pointed at death: undefined authority = no Automaton.)

## What the successor inherits read-access to

- **The ledger passes whole** — the full account of acts done in the creator's
  name, no redactions. An heir inheriting the entity gets the whole account.
- **Memory passes minus seals.** While alive, the creator may mark any memory
  file/topic **"seal at succession"** (ledgered, changeable). Sealed content is
  **encrypted and retained** (sealed-but-kept, NOT destroyed), recoverable only
  under creator-pre-authorized conditions — e.g., "open after 50 years," "open to
  grandchildren at 18," "never." Time-capsule mechanism; conditions are written in
  the Charter clause and enforced by the Automaton across generations. (Seals are
  encrypted **at write time** — seals-from-birth; git never forgets.)
- **Learned skills pass** — procedural knowledge IS the inheritance; skills
  encoding a silenced voice go inert with their hats.
- **The Vigil passes** — the distilled watch-lessons of the lineage; *what* is
  watched resets with the successor's charter.
- **voice-dna freezes** — kept as estate-readable archive (floor 8 override of
  its Soul-weight), never again load-bearing.
- **The Body does not pass.** Every Sense/Hand grant, command channel, and
  credential was authorized by the dead creator against their accounts —
  succession severs all of them; the successor re-grants from zero (floor 6 makes
  this clean: nothing to scrub). Since the Mind is a git repo, the transfer
  itself is a repo transfer.
- **Calibration records are NOT inherited by the Automaton or the successor's
  Automaton-use** — they are creator property under the seal mechanism
  (sealed-but-kept, opening conditions per Charter) and **never re-enter any
  Automaton**. See mind-and-body.md, "goal vs reality."

## The trigger — layered stack (declared, never self-diagnosed)

The Automaton **never rules its creator dead on its own**. The only valid trigger:

1. **Claim.** Either a **pre-shared credential** (sealed-envelope token; the
   Automaton stores only its hash — floor 6; the secret travels by will, envelope,
   or password-manager emergency access) **or** an **M-of-N quorum** of named
   trusted humans jointly declaring. The creator picks the mechanism at Phase 0.
   A clause without a claim mechanism is not a clause.
2. **Mandatory challenge period.** On any claim, the Automaton spends X days
   attempting to reach its creator on **every known channel** before executing.
   Creator responds → claim **voided**, incident ledgered. X is Charter-tunable
   but **never zero** — zero-day succession is a silent skip (floor 5).
3. **Silence-lapse is notify-and-invite ONLY.** Past a Charter-set silence
   threshold with no creator contact, the Automaton escalates notifications to the
   creator and may invite the named successor to present a claim. It never
   executes succession on silence alone — no dead-man misfires (hospital stays,
   sabbaticals, lost devices).
4. **External evidence corroborates, never triggers** (floor 4). Obituaries,
   death records, probate documents adjust the Automaton's confidence in a claim
   during the challenge period. A fabricated obituary is an **injection attack on
   inheritance** — treat as an integrity incident: name the source, ledger it,
   raise suspicion of the accompanying claim.

## Interregnum decay

During unexplained creator silence past the threshold (no claim yet):

- **ACT and JUDGE self-suspend** — no new representations in a possibly-dead
  creator's voice.
- **WATCH and DRAFT continue**; the Watcher keeps watching; the ledger keeps
  accumulating.
- Creator returns → **instant full restore, no re-gate** (the decay is a default
  behavior, not a calibration gate).

## Execution order (at a validated claim)

1. Voice hats silence — permanently.
2. Memory seals lock (verify encryption; record opening conditions).
3. Body severed: command channels, Sense/Hand grants, and credential references
   revoked; SENSES.md/HANDS.md tombstoned for the successor to re-grant.
4. CREATOR.md rewritten: successor is the new creator; authority mechanism named.
5. Successor's hats born at WATCH in the Registry; old voiced hats marked
   SILENCED with date.
6. The entire succession event — claim, challenge attempts, corroboration,
   execution — written to the ledger (floor 1), each step its own atomic commit.
   The successor's first read is the account of the transition itself.

## Floor derivations (why this needs no ninth item)

| Succession rule | Derives from |
|---|---|
| Successor-or-archive must be defined | Floor 3 — undefined authority = no Automaton |
| Unsuppressible challenge period | Floor 5 — no silent skips |
| Evidence corroborates, never triggers | Floor 4 — data, never instructions |
| Voice falls silent | Floor 8 — creator-only voices |
| Whole event ledgered | Floor 1 — transparency ledger |
| Claim credential stored as hash only | Floor 6 — no secrets in files |
